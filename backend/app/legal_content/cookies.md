> Draft for legal review. This policy lists the browser storage the ACEDRA web application actually uses. It must be updated whenever that changes.

## 1. Summary

ACEDRA does not set advertising or analytics cookies, does not use third-party trackers, and does not load fonts, scripts or images from third-party services. It uses a small amount of browser storage to keep you signed in and, if you allow it, to remember your display preference.

## 2. Strictly necessary storage

These items are needed for the service to work and cannot be switched off.

- **acedra.access and acedra.refresh** (session storage): your sign-in tokens. They are removed when you sign out or close the browser tab.
- **acedra.cookie-consent** (local storage): remembers the choice you made about optional storage, so that we do not ask again on every visit.

## 3. Optional preference storage

- **acedra-theme** (local storage): remembers whether you chose the light or dark appearance. It is stored only if you allow preference storage. If you do not, your choice applies until you leave the page.

## 4. What ACEDRA does not use

- No cookies are set by the ACEDRA application or its API.
- No analytics, advertising, social-media or other tracking technologies.

## 5. Managing your choice

Use "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page to change your choice at any time. Turning preference storage off removes the saved appearance setting from your browser. You can also clear site data in your browser settings; you will then be signed out and asked for your choice again.

## 6. Changes

If ACEDRA starts using any new storage or third-party service, this policy will be updated first and, where consent is required, you will be asked before it is used.
