## 3. Authorised institutional access

Each institution has its own separate workspace. Your access is limited to the workspace of the institution that invited you and to the records your role permits. Accounts are created by your institution's administrator, or for an institution's first administrator by the ACEDRA team, on email addresses from domains the institution has approved.

The ACEDRA team configures workspaces on an institution's behalf. Configuration access does not include access to student records. Any ACEDRA team access to student records requires a specific, time-limited grant that the institution has approved, and each such access is recorded in an audit log.

## 4. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity under your account. Tell your institution's administrator promptly if you believe your account has been misused. Your institution may change your role or disable your account at any time, which ends your active sessions.

## 5. Roles and permissions

What you can see and do depends on the role your institution gives you:

- Students see their own records, alerts, plan and readiness.
- Faculty see students in the sections they teach and students an administrator has explicitly shared with them.
- Mentors see the students assigned to them and the support cases they manage.
- University administrators manage their institution's users, structure, rules and data within their own workspace.
- Placement officers see placement-related information for their institution.
- ACEDRA team members configure workspaces and handle onboarding; they do not see student records without an approved grant.

## 6. Acceptable use

You must not:

- try to access another institution's workspace or records your role does not permit;
- share your account or let others use it;
- upload content you are not authorised to share, or content that is unlawful, harmful or infringes others' rights;
- interfere with the platform's security, performance or availability, or test its security without written permission;
- use the AI assistant to try to obtain information about people you are not authorised to see;
- use automated tools to extract data except through features the platform provides.

Early-warning alerts, recommendations and readiness scores are aids to support conversations. They must not be used as the sole basis for disciplinary, admission, assessment or other consequential decisions about a student.
